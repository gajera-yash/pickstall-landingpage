Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Welcome to Pickstall ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website at pickstall.store or use our creator platform and waitlist services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways:

  • Waitlist & Contact Information: When you sign up for our waitlist or contact us, we collect your email address and any details you provide.
  • Creator Account Data: When full accounts launch, we collect your name, username, store details, and profile information.
  • Payment & Transaction Information: Payment processing is handled directly and securely through Stripe Connect. We do not store full credit card numbers on our servers.
  • Device & Usage Data: We may automatically collect device type, browser information, IP address, and interaction data to improve site stability and performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Send waitlist updates, pre-launch invitations, and launch day announcements.
  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Pickstall creator storefront platform.
  • Process transactions and payout creator earnings via authorized processors (such as Stripe).
  • Respond to user support requests and improve our services.
  • Protect against fraudulent activity, spam, and security risks.

3. Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share data with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our platform:

  • Waitlist & Email Management: Services like GetLaunchList to manage our waitlist securely.
  • Payment Processing: Stripe for secure payments and creator payouts.
  • Hosting & Infrastructure: Reliable cloud hosting providers to keep our website secure.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to safeguard your personal data. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, no transmission over the Internet or electronic storage method is 100% secure.

5. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location (including under GDPR or CCPA), you may have the following rights:

  • The right to access, update, or delete your personal data.
  • The right to withdraw consent or unsubscribe from our waitlist emails at any time using the link in our emails.
  • The right to request a copy of the data we hold about you.
